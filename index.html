The Hook Rule

LinkedIn shows only the first 2–3 lines before "See more." Your opening has to stop the scroll. Bold statement or a challenge — never bury the lead.

Best Times to Post

  • Monday: 8–10am — people planning their week
  • Wednesday: 9–11am — peak mid-week
  • Friday: 8–10am — before the weekend drop

Hashtag Strategy

  • 5–8 hashtags per post
  • Always: #ConnectinEvents #EventManagement #CorporateEvents
  • Rotate supporting hashtags
  • Hashtags at the END only

Visuals = More Reach

Images and video get significantly more reach. Use real ConnectIn event photos — your work is visual gold. Behind-the-scenes shots beat stock every time.

Engage Fast

Reply to every comment in the first 60–90 mins. LinkedIn's algorithm rewards early engagement and pushes posts to more people.

Consistency Wins

3x per week, every week. Each month has its own 4-week cycle — stay on track and the results compound.
