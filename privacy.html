Privacy Policy

ConnectIn Events · Last updated: April 2026

This privacy policy applies to the ConnectIn Events Financial Dashboard ("the Application"), an internal tool used exclusively by ConnectIn Events Ltd staff to manage business operations.

1. Scope

The Application is not available to the general public. It is an internal business tool restricted to authorised ConnectIn Events employees and is protected by password authentication.

2. Information We Collect

The Application connects to ConnectIn Events' own QuickBooks Online account via Intuit's official API in order to display and manage our business's financial data. This data includes invoices, customers, payments, expenses, and reports that belong to ConnectIn Events Ltd.

The Application does not collect or store any personal data from end users beyond basic authentication tokens required for access control.

3. How We Use Information

Financial data retrieved from QuickBooks is displayed within the Application solely for the purpose of internal business reporting, invoice management, and operational oversight by authorised ConnectIn Events staff.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth authentication tokens issued by Intuit are stored securely as environment variables on our hosting provider (Vercel). The Application does not maintain its own database of customer or financial records — all data is fetched live from QuickBooks when needed.

5. Third Parties

The Application uses Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. Use of QuickBooks data is governed by Intuit's own privacy policy and terms. No financial data is shared with any third party outside of Intuit's platform.

6. Data Retention

We do not retain any QuickBooks data beyond what is displayed in active dashboard sessions. OAuth tokens can be revoked at any time via Intuit's developer settings.

7. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hello@connectinevents.co.uk.
